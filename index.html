About Annamalai University

The University

Annamalai University is a premier unitary and affiliating institution in Annamalainagar, Tamil Nadu, recognized for high academic standards and extensive research output across diverse fields.

Engineering Library

The FEAT Library serves as an academic nerve center for engineers, offering specialized book stacks in Computer Science, AI, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, and Chemical disciplines.

Question Paper Archive

This dedicated student portal brings together semester examination papers and syllabus study notes, helping scholars revise efficiently and succeed in their academic assessments.

Vision & Mission

Vision

Providing world class quality education with strong ethical values to nurture and develop outstanding professionals fit for a globally competitive environment.

Mission

  • Provide quality technical education with a sound footing on basic engineering principles and research capabilities.
  • Transform students into outstanding professionals capable of creating, developing, and managing global enterprises.
  • Develop a Global Knowledge Hub, striving continuously in pursuit of excellence in Education and Industry services.
  • Inculcate life-long learning to move forward with updated knowledge to face future technological challenges.
